ScreenKey Module × 6 — Breadboard Wiring

All 6 GPIO pin connections for the Waveshare 0.85" ScreenKey Module variant (9-pin SPI Control Interface) wired to the Pimoroni Pico Plus 2. Labels match firmware/pins.py and the Pinout table in hardware/README.md; colors match the wire harness in use (see the legend). Each Pico pin carries a breadboard coordinate: rows run C to V (20 rows, one per pin on each Pico edge, top to bottom) and columns run 1 to 8 left to right, so the left edge of the Pico sits in column 1 and the right edge in column 8 (e.g. GP0 is C1, VBUS is C8, GP15 is V1). Pin rows run top to bottom in the same physical order as the two 20-pin edges on the Pico Plus 2's silkscreen (docs/ppico_plus_2_pinout_diagram.pdf) — GND and 3V3 are wired from the top-right power pins. CLK (GP2) and DIN (GP7) sit on non-adjacent rows: both are still hardware SPI0 pins (SCK and TX), but GP3 — SPI0's other TX-capable pin — sat directly across from the crowded VCC/BULK row, so DIN moved to GP7 and swapped places with 4CS, which is a single wire and doesn't mind the crowding. BULK marks the single 10µF aluminum electrolytic cap at the point where 3V3/GND first land on the breadboard, bridging the rail before it fans out to all 6 modules — it is not a GPIO connection. Each module has its own onboard decoupling already, so no per-module cap is added here. This part is polarized: the shorter/banded lead is negative and goes to GND, the long lead to 3V3.
