ScreenKey Module × 6 — Breadboard Wiring

All 6 GPIO pin connections for the Waveshare 0.85" ScreenKey Module variant (9-pin SPI Control Interface) wired to the Pimoroni Pico Plus 2. Colors and labels match firmware/pins.py and the Pinout table in hardware/README.md. Pin rows run top to bottom in the same physical order as the two 20-pin edges on the Pico Plus 2's silkscreen (docs/ppico_plus_2_pinout_diagram.pdf) — GND and 3V3 are wired from the top-right power pins.
